### 系统广义坐标与约束方程
系统广义坐标 $\boldsymbol{q}$ 由系统派生树中非切断铰元件的相对运动广义坐标组成，并满足由切断铰元件引起的闭环约束方程 $\boldsymbol{0} = \boldsymbol{c}(\boldsymbol{q})$, 可分别表示为
$$
\boldsymbol{q}=\left[\begin{array}{c}
\boldsymbol{q}_{\mathrm{J}_{1}} \\
\boldsymbol{q}_{\mathrm{J}_{2}} \\
\vdots \\
\boldsymbol{q}_{\mathrm{J}_{n}}
\end{array}\right], \quad \boldsymbol{c}=\left[\begin{array}{c}
c_{\mathrm{L}_{1}} \\
c_{\mathrm{L}_{2}} \\
\vdots \\
c_{\mathrm{L}_{m}}
\end{array}\right]
$$
>此处存疑:闭环约束方程究竟满足怎样的关系？切断铰元件引起的闭环约束方程属于前文的自身约束还是铰元件约束？

式中 $n$为非切断铰元件的个数（等于系统包含的体元件个数）；$m$ 为切断铰元件的个数（等于系统中闭环的个数）

>后者不难理解，有一个闭环就需要切断一个铰元件。但是前者？为什么非切断铰元件个数就是系统包含的体元件个数？
>答：一个结点一定拖着一个枝。

对于前面的四足机器人多刚体系统动力学模型：$n=21,m=4$.

## 相邻体元件运动学量递推关系
### 相邻体元件 位置级运动学量递推关系
以 $O_{i}$为原点的体元件连体坐标系简称为$i$系. 将大地元件连体坐标系, 即 0 系, 视为全局惯性坐标系，则 $i(i = 1, 2, \cdots, n)$ 系在全局惯性坐标系中的位置和方位可通过递推的方式表示为
$$\left.\left\{\begin{array}{rl}\boldsymbol{r}_{\mathrm{O}_0\mathrm{O}_i|0}&=\boldsymbol{r}_{\mathrm{O}_0\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}|0}+ \boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}\boldsymbol{r}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}\mathrm{O}_i|\mathrm{p}(i)},\\\boldsymbol{A}_{0,i}&=\boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i},\end{array}\right.\right.$$
式中
- $\boldsymbol{r}_{\mathrm{O}_0\mathrm{O}_i|0}$表示从 $\mathrm{P}_{1}$ 点到  $\mathrm{P}_{2}$ 的位置矢量在 $i$ 系中的投影
- $\boldsymbol{A}_{i,j}$表示从 $j$ 系到 $i$ 系的坐标变换矩阵，

$\boldsymbol{r}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}\mathrm{O}_{i}|\mathrm{P}(i)}$ 又可表示为
$$\boldsymbol{r}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}\mathrm{O}_{i}|\mathrm{P}(i)} = \boldsymbol{r}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}\mathrm{R}_{i}|\mathrm{P}(i)} + \boldsymbol{r}_{\mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}\mathrm{O}_{i}|\mathrm{P}(i)}$$
两边求导。
![[SystemDynamicsModelingAndSimulation_92.png||3000]]
$$\dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}\mathrm{O}_{i}|\mathrm{P}(i)} = \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}\mathrm{R}_{i}|\mathrm{P}(i)} + \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}\mathrm{O}_{i}|\mathrm{P}(i)}$$
因为$\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)},\mathrm{R}_{i}$ 均为$\mathrm{P}_{i}$系中的点，两者之间的向量$\boldsymbol{r}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}\mathrm{R}_{i}|\mathrm{P}(i)}$不随时间发生变化，即$\boldsymbol{r}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}\mathrm{R}_{i}|\mathrm{P}(i)}$定常，于是$\dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}\mathrm{R}_{i}|\mathrm{P}(i)}=0$.

上述等式中$\boldsymbol{r}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}\mathrm{O}_{i}|\mathrm{P}(i)}$和$\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}$等均可表示为广义坐标$\boldsymbol{q}_{J_{i}}$的表达式。

### 相邻体元件速度级运动学量递推关系
将 $j$ 系相对于 $i$ 系的相对平动速度和相对转动速度在 $j$ 系中的六维投影列阵记为 $\boldsymbol{v}_{i,j|j}$，即
$$\boldsymbol{v}_{i, j \mid j} \triangleq\left[\begin{array}{c}
 \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_{i} \mathrm{O}_{j} \mid j} \\
\boldsymbol{\omega}_{i, j \mid j}
\end{array}\right]=
\left[\begin{array}{c}
\boldsymbol{A}_{i, j}^{\mathrm{T}} \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_{i} \mathrm{O}_{j} \mid i} \\
\boldsymbol{\omega}_{i, j \mid j}
\end{array}\right]$$
其中：
- $\boldsymbol{\omega}_{i, j \mid j}$表示$j$系相对于$i$系的相对角速度矢量在$j$系中的投影；
- $\dot{\boldsymbol{r}}$表示$\boldsymbol{r}$对时间的一阶导数。
>为什么不统一采用在$j$系中的表达


相邻元件速度运动学量递推表达式可记为：
$$\boldsymbol{v}_{0,i\mid i}=\boldsymbol{C}_{i,\mathrm{p}(i)}\boldsymbol{v}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)\mid \mathrm{p}(i)}+\boldsymbol{\Phi}_{J_{i}}\dot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{J_{i}}$$
下面给出这个式子的证明：
由速度叠加定理或对位置级运动学方程
$$\left.\left\{\begin{array}{rl}\boldsymbol{r}_{\mathrm{O}_0\mathrm{O}_i|0}&=\boldsymbol{r}_{\mathrm{O}_0\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}|0}+ \boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}\boldsymbol{r}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}\mathrm{O}_i|\mathrm{p}(i)},\\\boldsymbol{A}_{0,i}&=\boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i},\end{array}\right.\right.$$
求时间导数，

先对第一个等式进行求导：
$$\boldsymbol{r}_{\mathrm{O}_0\mathrm{O}_i|0}=\boldsymbol{r}_{\mathrm{O}_0\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}|0}+ \boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}\boldsymbol{r}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}\mathrm{O}_i|\mathrm{p}(i)}$$
$$\dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_0\mathrm{O}_i|0}=\dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_0\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}|0}+ \dot{\boldsymbol{A}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}\boldsymbol{r}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}\mathrm{O}_i|\mathrm{p}(i)}+\boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}\dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}\mathrm{O}_i|\mathrm{p}(i)}$$
然后由前面 [[7读书笔记——第七章 坐标系的姿态广义坐标]] 中的式子：
$$\tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{ij|j} = \boldsymbol{A}_{ij}^{\mathrm{T}}\dot{\boldsymbol{A}}_{ij}$$
有
$$\dot{\boldsymbol{A}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}=\boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}\tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)\mid \mathrm{p}(i)}$$
于是上式可化为：
$$\dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_0\mathrm{O}_i|0}=\dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_0\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}|0}+ \boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}\tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)\mid \mathrm{p}(i)}\boldsymbol{r}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}\mathrm{O}_i|\mathrm{p}(i)}+\boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}\dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}\mathrm{O}_i|\mathrm{p}(i)}$$
利用反对称矩阵性质 $\tilde{\boldsymbol{a}}\boldsymbol{b}=\boldsymbol{a}\times\boldsymbol{b}=-\boldsymbol{b}\times\boldsymbol{a}=\tilde{\boldsymbol{b}}^{\mathrm{T}}\boldsymbol{a}$，即
$$\tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}\,\boldsymbol{r}=\tilde{\boldsymbol{r}}^{\mathrm{T}}\,\boldsymbol{\omega}$$
可化为
$$\dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_0\mathrm{O}_i|0}=
\dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_0\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}|0}+
\boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}\tilde{\boldsymbol{r}}^{\mathrm{T}}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}\mathrm{O}_i|\mathrm{p}(i)}\boldsymbol{\omega}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)\mid \mathrm{p}(i)}+
\boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}\dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}\mathrm{O}_i|\mathrm{p}(i)}$$
再看第二个式子。
$$\boldsymbol{A}_{0,i}=\boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}$$

两边对时间求导（乘积法则）：
$$\dot{\boldsymbol{A}}_{0,i}=\dot{\boldsymbol{A}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}+\boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}\dot{\boldsymbol{A}}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}$$
代入 $\dot{\boldsymbol{A}}_{ij}=\boldsymbol{A}_{ij}\tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{ij|j}$，得
$$\boldsymbol{A}_{0,i}\tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,i\mid i}=\boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}\tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)\mid \mathrm{p}(i)}\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}+\boldsymbol{A}_{0,i}\tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i\mid i}$$
利用恒等式 $\tilde{\boldsymbol{v}}_{|A}=\boldsymbol{A}_{AB}\tilde{\boldsymbol{v}}_{|B} \boldsymbol{A}_{AB}^\mathrm{T}$（证明见 [[5读书笔记——第五章 坐标系的相对方位]] 性质七），两边同乘$\boldsymbol{A}_{i,0}$：
$$\boldsymbol{A}_{0,i}\tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,i\mid i}\boldsymbol{A}_{i,0}=
\boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}\tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)\mid \mathrm{p}(i)}\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}\boldsymbol{A}_{i,0}+
\boldsymbol{A}_{0,i}\tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i\mid i}\boldsymbol{A}_{i,0}$$

注意到每个形如 $\boldsymbol{A}\tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}\boldsymbol{A}^{\mathrm{T}}$ 的项，按性质七 $\tilde{\boldsymbol{v}}_{|A}=\boldsymbol{A}_{AB}\tilde{\boldsymbol{v}}_{|B}\boldsymbol{A}_{AB}^{\mathrm{T}}$ 恰好就是该角速度在 0 系中的叉乘矩阵；这里用到 $\boldsymbol{A}_{i,0}=\boldsymbol{A}_{0,i}^{\mathrm{T}}$ 及 $\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}\boldsymbol{A}_{i,0}=\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),0}=\boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}^{\mathrm{T}}$：
$$\boldsymbol{A}_{0,i}\tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,i\mid i}\boldsymbol{A}_{0,i}^{\mathrm{T}}=\tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,i\mid 0},\qquad
\boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}\tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)\mid \mathrm{p}(i)}\boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}^{\mathrm{T}}=\tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)\mid 0},\qquad
\boldsymbol{A}_{0,i}\tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i\mid i}\boldsymbol{A}_{0,i}^{\mathrm{T}}=\tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i\mid 0}$$
代入上式，即化为

$$\tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,i\mid 0}=
\tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)\mid 0}+
\tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i\mid 0}$$

去掉波浪号（向量与反对称矩阵一一对应），得 0 系下的角速度矢量叠加：
$$\boldsymbol{\omega}_{0,i\mid 0}=\boldsymbol{\omega}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)\mid 0}+\boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i\mid 0}$$
再按 $\boldsymbol{v}_{|0}=\boldsymbol{A}_{0,j}\boldsymbol{v}_{|j}$ 把各项投影到各自的连体坐标系：
$$\boldsymbol{\omega}_{0,i\mid 0}=\boldsymbol{A}_{0,i}\boldsymbol{\omega}_{0,i\mid i},\qquad
\boldsymbol{\omega}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)\mid 0}=\boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}\boldsymbol{\omega}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)\mid \mathrm{p}(i)},\qquad
\boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i\mid 0}=\boldsymbol{A}_{0,i}\boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i\mid i}=\boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}\boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i\mid i}$$

即得
$$\boldsymbol{A}_{0, i} \boldsymbol{\omega}_{0, i \mid i}  =\boldsymbol{A}_{0, \mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{\omega}_{0, \mathrm{p}(i) \mid \mathrm{p}(i)}+\boldsymbol{A}_{0, \mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i} \boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i}$$

整理一下可得速度级运动学方程，即：
$$\left\{\begin{aligned}
\dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_{0} \mathrm{O}_{i} \mid 0} & =\dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_{0} \mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)} \mid 0}+\boldsymbol{A}_{0, \mathrm{p}(i)} \tilde{\boldsymbol{r}}^{\mathrm{T}}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)} \mathrm{O}_{i} \mid \mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{\omega}_{0, \mathrm{p}(i) \mid \mathrm{p}(i)} \\
& +\boldsymbol{A}_{0, \mathrm{p}(i)} \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)} \mathrm{O} i \mid \mathrm{p}(i)} \\
\boldsymbol{A}_{0, i} \boldsymbol{\omega}_{0, i \mid i} & =\boldsymbol{A}_{0, \mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{\omega}_{0, \mathrm{p}(i) \mid \mathrm{p}(i)}+\boldsymbol{A}_{0, \mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i} \boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i} .
\end{aligned}\right.$$
可进一步整理为：
$$\begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{A}_{0,i}^{\mathrm{T}} \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_0 O_i|0} \\ \boldsymbol{\omega}_{0,i|i} \end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} & \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \tilde{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_{\mathrm{p}(i)} O_i|i}^{\mathrm{T}} \\ \boldsymbol{O}_3 & \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}^{\mathrm{T}} \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_0 O_{\mathrm{p}(i)}|0} \\ \boldsymbol{\omega}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|i} \end{bmatrix}
+
\begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_{\mathrm{p}(i)} O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)} \\ \boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|i} \end{bmatrix},$$
定义 $\boldsymbol{C}_{i,\mathrm{p}(i)}$ 为：
$$\boldsymbol{C}_{i,\mathrm{p}(i)}=\begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} & \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \tilde{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_{\mathrm{p}(i)} O_i|i}^{\mathrm{T}} \\ \boldsymbol{O}_3 & \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \end{bmatrix}$$
则可进一步简记为：
$$\boldsymbol{v}_{0,i|i} = \boldsymbol{C}_{i,\mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{v}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} + \boldsymbol{v}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|i}.$$
然后捏，邻接体相对速度$\boldsymbol{v}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|i}$由于$\dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}\mathrm{O}_{i}|\mathrm{P}(i)} =  \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{p}(i)}\mathrm{O}_{i}|\mathrm{P}(i)}$
有
$$\boldsymbol{v}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|i} = \begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_{\mathrm{p}(i)} O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)} \\ \boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|i} \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{R_{\mathrm{p}(i)} O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)} \\ \boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|i} \end{bmatrix}$$
这个式子一定与$\boldsymbol{q}_{J_{i}}$线性相关，下一节面将会解答


该线性关系可表示为：
$$\boldsymbol{v}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|i} = \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_i} \dot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_i}$$
### 相邻体元件加速度运动学量递推关系
将 $j$ 系相对于 $i$ 系的相对平动加速度和相对转动加速度在 $j$ 系中的六维投影列阵记为 $\boldsymbol{a}_{i,j|j}$，即
$$
\boldsymbol{a}_{i,j|j} \triangleq \begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{A}_{i,j}^{\mathrm{T}} \ddot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_i O_j|i} \\ \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{i,j|j} \end{bmatrix},
$$
相邻元件加速度运动学量递推表达式可记为
$$
\boldsymbol{a}_{0,i|i} = \boldsymbol{C}_{i,\mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{a}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} + \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_i} \ddot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_i} + \boldsymbol{\eta}_i,
$$
式中
$$
\boldsymbol{\eta}_i = \boldsymbol{\xi}_{\mathrm{J}_i} + \begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \left( \tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{r}_{O_{\mathrm{p}(i)} O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)} + 2 \tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_{\mathrm{p}(i)} O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)} \right) \\ \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|\mathrm{p}(i)} \end{bmatrix},
$$
式中各类铰元件相对加速度非齐次项 $\boldsymbol{\xi}_{\mathrm{J}_i}$ 的具体表达式见下一小节。 

下面开始证明：
有速度级运动方程：
$$\left\{\begin{aligned}
\dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_{0} \mathrm{O}_{i} \mid 0} & =\dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_{0} \mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)} \mid 0}+
\boldsymbol{A}_{0, \mathrm{p}(i)} \tilde{\boldsymbol{r}}^{\mathrm{T}}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)} \mathrm{O}_{i} \mid \mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{\omega}_{0, \mathrm{p}(i) \mid \mathrm{p}(i)} \\
& +\boldsymbol{A}_{0, \mathrm{p}(i)} \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)} \mathrm{O} i \mid \mathrm{p}(i)} \\
\boldsymbol{A}_{0, i} \boldsymbol{\omega}_{0, i \mid i} & =\boldsymbol{A}_{0, \mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{\omega}_{0, \mathrm{p}(i) \mid \mathrm{p}(i)}+\boldsymbol{A}_{0, \mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i} \boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i} .
\end{aligned}\right.$$
对时间再求一阶导数，可得下式。第一次推导过程中的唯一一个阻碍就是：
$$\boldsymbol{A}_{0, \mathrm{p}(i)}\dot{\tilde{\boldsymbol{r}}}^{\mathrm{T}}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)} \mathrm{O}_{i} \mid \mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{\omega}_{0, \mathrm{p}(i) \mid \mathrm{p}(i)} =\boldsymbol{A}_{0, \mathrm{p}(i)}(\boldsymbol{\omega}_{0, \mathrm{p}(i) \mid \mathrm{p}(i)}\times \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)} \mathrm{O}_{i} \mid \mathrm{p}(i)})$$
其他应该畅通无阻。如果未来的我自己推不出来，要打屁股了。
$$\begin{cases}
\ddot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_0 O_i|0} = \ddot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_0 O_{\mathrm{p}(i)}|0} + \boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)} \tilde{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_{\mathrm{p}(i)} O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)}^{\mathrm{T}} \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \\
\quad + \boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)} \ddot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_{\mathrm{p}(i)} O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)} \\
\quad + \boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)} \tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{r}_{O_{\mathrm{p}(i)} O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)} \\
\quad + 2 \boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)} \tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_{\mathrm{p}(i)} O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)}, \\[6pt]
\boldsymbol{A}_{0,i} \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,i|i} = \boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)} \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \\
\quad + \boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i} \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|i} \\
\quad + \boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)} \tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i} \boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|i}.
\end{cases}$$
以上两式两端分别左乘 $\boldsymbol{A}_{0,i} = \boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}$ 的转置，整理可得
$$\begin{cases}
\boldsymbol{A}_{0,i}^{\mathrm{T}} \ddot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_0 O_i|0} = \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}^{\mathrm{T}} \ddot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_0 O_{\mathrm{p}(i)}|0} + \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \tilde{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_{\mathrm{p}(i)} O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)}^{\mathrm{T}} \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \\
\quad + \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \ddot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_{\mathrm{p}(i)} O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)} \\
\quad + \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{r}_{O_{\mathrm{p}(i)} O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)} \\
\quad + 2 \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_{\mathrm{p}(i)} O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)}, \\[6pt]
\dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,i|i} = \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \\
\quad + \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|i} \\
\quad + \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|i}.
\end{cases}$$
将以上两式写成矩阵形式

$$
\begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{A}_{0,i}^{\mathrm{T}} \ddot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_0 O_i|0} \\ \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,i|i} \end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} & \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \tilde{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_{\mathrm{p}(i)} O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)}^{\mathrm{T}} \\ \boldsymbol{O}_3 & \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{A}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)}^{\mathrm{T}} \ddot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_0 O_{\mathrm{p}(i)}|0} \\ \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \end{bmatrix}
+
\begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \ddot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_{\mathrm{p}(i)} O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)} \\ \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|i} \end{bmatrix}
+
\begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \left( \tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{r}_{O_{\mathrm{p}(i)} O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)} + 2 \tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_{\mathrm{p}(i)} O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)} \right) \\ \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|\mathrm{p}(i)} \end{bmatrix}
$$


并可进一步简记为
$$
\boldsymbol{a}_{0,i|i} = \boldsymbol{C}_{i,\mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{a}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} + \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_i} \ddot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_i} + \boldsymbol{\eta}_i.
$$
邻接体相对加速度
$$
\boldsymbol{a}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|i} = \begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \ddot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_{\mathrm{p}(i)} O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)} \\ \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|i} \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \ddot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{R_i O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)} \\ \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|i} \end{bmatrix},
$$
一定与 $\ddot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_i}$ 线性相关，该线性关系可表示为
$$
\boldsymbol{a}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|i} = \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_i} \ddot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_i} + \boldsymbol{\xi}_{\mathrm{J}_i},
$$
式中加速度系数矩阵 $\boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_i}$ 与之前的的速度系数矩阵 $\boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_i}$ 相同。将上式代入整理可得
$$
\boldsymbol{a}_{0,i|i} = \boldsymbol{C}_{i,\mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{a}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} + \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_i} \ddot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_i} + \boldsymbol{\eta}_i,
$$
式中
$$
\boldsymbol{\eta}_i = \boldsymbol{\xi}_{\mathrm{J}_i} + \begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \left( \tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{r}_{O_{\mathrm{p}(i)} O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)} + 2 \tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_{\mathrm{p}(i)} O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)} \right) \\ \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \tilde{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)|\mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|\mathrm{p}(i)} \end{bmatrix}.
$$

式中各类铰元件相对加速度非齐次项$\boldsymbol{\xi}_{\mathrm{J}_i}$的具体表达式见下一小节。

### 主铰元件相对运动学方程
**虚拟铰**：为了方便数学递推而"构造"出来的铰。它的作用是把两个相邻刚体之间的相对位形（位置和姿态）分解为一系列基本运动，使得递推公式能够统一表达。并不提供实际约束。
对于虚拟铰元件 $i$, 位置级相对运动学方程可表示为
$$
\begin{cases}
\boldsymbol{r}_{R_i O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)} = \boldsymbol{l}_{\mathrm{J}_i},\\[6pt]
\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i} = \boldsymbol{A}_Z(\theta_{\mathrm{J}_i,1}) \boldsymbol{A}_Y(\theta_{\mathrm{J}_i,2}) \boldsymbol{A}_X(\theta_{\mathrm{J}_i,3}),
\end{cases}
$$
式中 $[x_{\mathrm{J}_i}\ y_{\mathrm{J}_i}\ z_{\mathrm{J}_i}]^{\mathrm{T}} =: \boldsymbol{l}_{\mathrm{J}_i}$ 和 $[\theta_{\mathrm{J}_i,1}\ \theta_{\mathrm{J}_i,2}\ \theta_{\mathrm{J}_i,3}]^{\mathrm{T}} =: \boldsymbol{\theta}_{\mathrm{J}_i}$ 分别为描述虚拟铰元件 $i$ 平动和转动的广义坐标, 绕坐标轴的单位旋转矩阵表达式分别为
$$
\boldsymbol{A}_X(\theta) = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & \cos\theta & -\sin\theta \\ 0 & \sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{bmatrix},
\boldsymbol{A}_Y(\theta) = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta & 0 & \sin\theta \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ -\sin\theta & 0 & \cos\theta \end{bmatrix},
\boldsymbol{A}_Z(\theta) = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta & -\sin\theta & 0 \\ \sin\theta & \cos\theta & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}.
$$速度级相对运动学方程可表示为（角速度见原书“相对运动的角速度一节”）
$$
\begin{cases}
\dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{R_i O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)} = \dot{\boldsymbol{l}}_{\mathrm{J}_i},\\[6pt]
\boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|i} = \boldsymbol{H}_{\mathrm{B321}}(\boldsymbol{\theta}_{\mathrm{J}_i}) \dot{\boldsymbol{\theta}}_{\mathrm{J}_i},
\end{cases}
$$
由此可得
$$
\begin{aligned}
\boldsymbol{v}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|i} &= \begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_{\mathrm{p}(i)} O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)} \\ \boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|i} \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{R_i O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)} \\ \boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|i} \end{bmatrix} \\
&= \begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} & \boldsymbol{O}_3 \\ \boldsymbol{O}_3 & \boldsymbol{H}_{\mathrm{B321}}(\boldsymbol{\theta}_{\mathrm{J}_i}) \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \dot{\boldsymbol{l}}_{\mathrm{J}_i} \\ \dot{\boldsymbol{\theta}}_{\mathrm{J}_i} \end{bmatrix} \\
&=: \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_i} \dot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_i}.
\end{aligned}
$$
加速度相对运动学方程可表示为
$$
\begin{cases}
\ddot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{R_i O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)} = \ddot{\boldsymbol{l}}_{\mathrm{J}_i},\\[6pt]
\dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|i} = \boldsymbol{H}_{\mathrm{B321}}(\boldsymbol{\theta}_{\mathrm{J}_i}) \ddot{\boldsymbol{\theta}}_{\mathrm{J}_i} + \dot{\boldsymbol{H}}_{\mathrm{B321}}(\boldsymbol{\theta}_{\mathrm{J}_i}, \dot{\boldsymbol{\theta}}_{\mathrm{J}_i}) \dot{\boldsymbol{\theta}}_{\mathrm{J}_i},
\end{cases}
$$
由此可得
$$
\begin{aligned}
\boldsymbol{a}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|i}
&= \begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \ddot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{O_{\mathrm{p}(i)} O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)} \\ \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|i} \end{bmatrix}
 = \begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} \ddot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{R_i O_i|\mathrm{p}(i)} \\ \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i|i} \end{bmatrix} \\
&= \begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i),i}^{\mathrm{T}} & \boldsymbol{O}_3 \\ \boldsymbol{O}_3 & \boldsymbol{H}_{\mathrm{B321}}(\boldsymbol{\theta}_{\mathrm{J}_i}) \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \ddot{\boldsymbol{l}}_{\mathrm{J}_i} \\ \ddot{\boldsymbol{\theta}}_{\mathrm{J}_i} \end{bmatrix}
 + \begin{bmatrix} \boldsymbol{0}_3 \\ \dot{\boldsymbol{H}}_{\mathrm{B321}}(\boldsymbol{\theta}_{\mathrm{J}_i}, \dot{\boldsymbol{\theta}}_{\mathrm{J}_i}) \dot{\boldsymbol{\theta}}_{\mathrm{J}_i} \end{bmatrix} \\
&=: \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_i} \ddot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_i} + \boldsymbol{\xi}_{\mathrm{J}_i}.
\end{aligned}
$$
上述等式中
$$
\boldsymbol{H}_{\mathrm{B321}}(\boldsymbol{\theta})
=
\begin{bmatrix}
-s_2 & 0 & 1 \\
c_2 s_3 & c_3 & 0 \\
c_2 c_3 & -s_3 & 0
\end{bmatrix},
\quad
\dot{\boldsymbol{H}}_{\mathrm{B321}}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
-c_2 \dot{\theta}_2 & 0 & 0 \\
-s_2 s_3 \dot{\theta}_2 + c_2 c_3 \dot{\theta}_3 & -s_3 \dot{\theta}_3 & 0 \\
-s_2 c_3 \dot{\theta}_2 - c_2 s_3 \dot{\theta}_3 & -c_3 \dot{\theta}_3 & 0
\end{bmatrix},
$$
式中 $c_2 = \cos(\theta_2)$, $s_2 = \sin(\theta_2)$, $c_3 = \cos(\theta_3)$, $s_3 = \sin(\theta_3)$.

由此，我们得到了虚拟铰的相对运动学方程。下面可以讨论基本的铰元件了。

### 转动铰元件
对于转动铰元件，我们知道其约束三个平动自由度，约束两个转动自由度。于是我们可以把位置级相对运动学方程可表示为
$$\left\{\begin{aligned}
\boldsymbol{r}_{\mathrm{R}_{i} \mathrm{O}_{i} \mid \mathrm{p}(i)} & =\mathbf{0}_{3}, \\
\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i} & =\boldsymbol{I}_{3} \cos \theta_{\mathrm{J}_{i}}+\tilde{\boldsymbol{n}}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} \sin \theta_{\mathrm{J}_{i}}+\boldsymbol{n}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} \boldsymbol{n}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}}^{\mathrm{T}}\left(1-\cos \theta_{\mathrm{J}_{i}}\right) \\
& =I_{3}+\tilde{n}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} \sin \theta_{\mathrm{J}_{i}}+\tilde{n}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} \tilde{n}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}}\left(1-\cos \theta_{\mathrm{J}_{i}}\right),
\end{aligned}\right.$$


式中  $\boldsymbol{n}_{J_{i}}$  表示转动铰元件  $i$  沿转轴方向的单位矢量在  $i$  系和  $\mathrm{p}(i)$  系中的投影列阵。

对位移级相对运动学方程对时间求导，可得
速度级相对运动学方程
$$\left\{\begin{aligned}
\dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{R}_{i} \mathrm{O}_{i} \mid \mathrm{p}(i)} & =\mathbf{0}_{3}, \\
\boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i} & =\boldsymbol{n}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} \dot{\theta}_{\mathrm{J}_{j}},
\end{aligned}\right.$$
由此可得

$$
\begin{aligned}
\boldsymbol{v}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i} & =\left[\begin{array}{c}
\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i}^{\mathrm{T}} \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)} \mathrm{O}_{i}\mid \mathrm{p}(i)}   \\
\boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i}
\end{array}\right] \\
& =\left[\begin{array}{c}
\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i}^{\mathrm{T}} \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{R} i\mathrm{O}_{i} \mid \mathrm{p}(i) } \\
\boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i}
\end{array}\right] \\
& =\left[\begin{array}{c}
\mathbf{0}_{3} \\
\boldsymbol{n}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}}
\end{array}\right] \dot{\theta}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} \\
& =: \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} \dot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} .
\end{aligned}
$$


加速度相对运动学方程可表示为
$$
\left\{\begin{aligned}
\ddot{r}_{\mathrm{R}_{i} \mathrm{O}\mid \mathrm{lp}} (i) & =\mathbf{0}_{3} \\
\dot{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i} & =\boldsymbol{n}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} \ddot{\theta}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}},
\end{aligned}\right.
$$


由此可得
$$
\begin{aligned}
\boldsymbol{a}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i} & =\left[\begin{array}{c}
\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i}^{\mathrm{T}} \ddot{r}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)} \mathrm{O}_{i} \mid \mathrm{p}(i)} \\
\dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i}
\end{array}\right] \\
& =\left[\begin{array}{c}
\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i}^{\mathrm{T}} \ddot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{R}_{i} \mathrm{O}_{i} \mid \mathrm{p}(i)} \\
\dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i}
\end{array}\right] \\
& =\left[\begin{array}{c}
\mathbf{0}_{3} \\
\boldsymbol{n}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}}
\end{array}\right] \ddot{\theta}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} \\
& =: \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} \ddot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}}+\xi_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} .
\end{aligned}
$$
>这里的$\xi_{\mathrm{J}_{i}}$为0？

### 棱柱铰元件

根据约束关系可得位置级相对运动方程：
$$\left\{\begin{aligned}
\boldsymbol{r}_{\mathrm{R}_{i} \mathrm{O}_{i} \mid \mathrm{p}(i)} & =\boldsymbol{n}_{J_{i}}\boldsymbol{l}_{J_{i}}, \\
\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i} & =\boldsymbol{I}_{3} 
\end{aligned}\right.$$
对位置级相对运动方程对时间求导可得：
$$\left\{\begin{aligned}
\dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{R}_{i} \mathrm{O}_{i} \mid \mathrm{p}(i)} & =\boldsymbol{n}_{J_{i}}\dot{\boldsymbol{l}}_{J_{i}}, \\
\boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i} & =\boldsymbol{0}
\end{aligned}\right.$$
由此可得
$$
\begin{aligned}
\boldsymbol{v}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i} & =\left[\begin{array}{c}
\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i}^{\mathrm{T}} \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{p}(i)} \mathrm{O}_{i}\mid \mathrm{p}(i)}   \\
\boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i}
\end{array}\right] \\
& =\left[\begin{array}{c}
\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i}^{\mathrm{T}} \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{R} i\mathrm{O}_{i} \mid \mathrm{p}(i) } \\
\boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i}
\end{array}\right] \\
& =\left[\begin{array}{c}
 \boldsymbol{n}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}}\\\mathbf{0}_{3}
\end{array}\right] \dot{\boldsymbol{l}}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} \\
& =: \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} \dot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} .
\end{aligned}
$$
再求一次导数，可得加速度相对运动学方程为：
$$\left\{\begin{aligned}
\ddot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{R}_{i} \mathrm{O}_{i} \mid \mathrm{p}(i)} & =\boldsymbol{n}_{J_{i}}\ddot{\boldsymbol{l}}_{J_{i}}, \\
\dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i\mid i} & =\boldsymbol{0} _{3}
\end{aligned}\right.$$
由此可得
$$\begin{aligned}
\boldsymbol{a}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i} & =\left[\begin{array}{c}

\dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i}
\end{array}\right] \\

\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i}^{\mathrm{T}} \ddot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{R}_{i} \mathrm{O}_{i} \mid \mathrm{p}(i)} \\
\dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i}
\end{array}\right] \\
& =\left[\begin{array}{c}
 \boldsymbol{n}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}}\\\mathbf{0}_{3}
\end{array}\right] \ddot{\boldsymbol{l}}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}}\\
& =: \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} \ddot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}}+\xi_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} .
\end{aligned}$$
> $\xi_{\mathrm{J}_{i}}$依旧为0？

### 球铰元件
球铰限制三个平动自由度，不限制转动自由度。
对于球铰元件 $i$, 位置级相对运动学方程可表示为
$$\left\{\begin{aligned}
\boldsymbol{r}_{\mathrm{R}_{i} \mathrm{O}_{i} \mid \mathrm{p}(i)} & =\boldsymbol{0}_{3}, \\
\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i} & =\boldsymbol{A}_{Z} (\theta_{J_{i},1})\boldsymbol{A}_{Y} (\theta_{J_{i},2})\boldsymbol{A}_{X} (\theta_{J_{i},3})
\end{aligned}\right.$$
速度级相对运动学方程可表示为
$$\left\{\begin{aligned}
\dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{R}_{i} \mathrm{O}_{i}\mid \mathrm{p}(i)}  & =\mathbf{0} \\
\boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i} & =\boldsymbol{H}_{\mathrm{B} 321}\left(\boldsymbol{\theta}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}}\right) \dot{\boldsymbol{\theta}}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}},
\end{aligned}\right.$$
由此可得
$$\begin{aligned}
\boldsymbol{v}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i} & =\left[\begin{array}{c}
\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i}^{\mathrm{T}} \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O} \mathrm{p}(i)} \mathrm{O}_{i} \mid \mathrm{p}(i) \\
\boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i}
\end{array}\right]=\left[\begin{array}{c}
\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i}^{\mathrm{T}} \dot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{R}_{i} \mathrm{O}} \mid \mathrm{p}(i) \\
\boldsymbol{\omega}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i}
\end{array}\right] \\
& =\left[\begin{array}{c}
\boldsymbol{O}_{3} \\
\boldsymbol{H}_{\mathrm{B} 321}\left(\boldsymbol{\theta}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}}\right)
\end{array}\right] \dot{\boldsymbol{\theta}}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} \\
& =: \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} \dot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} .
\end{aligned}$$
加速度相对运动学方程可表示为
$$\left\{\begin{aligned}
\ddot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{R}_{i} \mathrm{O}_{i}\mid \mathrm{p}(i)}  & =\mathbf{0} \\
\dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i} & =\boldsymbol{H}_{\mathrm{B} 321}\left(\boldsymbol{\theta}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}}\right) \ddot{\boldsymbol{\theta}}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}}+\dot{\boldsymbol{H}}_{\mathrm{B} 321}\left(\boldsymbol{\theta}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}}, \dot{\boldsymbol{\theta}}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}}\right) \dot{\boldsymbol{\theta}}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}},
\end{aligned}\right.$$

由此可得：
$$\begin{aligned}
\boldsymbol{a}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i} & =\left[\begin{array}{c}
\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i}^{\mathrm{T}} \ddot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O} \mathrm{p}(i)} \mathrm{O}_{i} \mid \mathrm{p}(i) \\
\dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i}
\end{array}\right]=\left[\begin{array}{c}
\boldsymbol{A}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i}^{\mathrm{T}} \ddot{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{R}_{i} \mathrm{O}} \mid \mathrm{p}(i) \\
\dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{\mathrm{p}(i), i \mid i}
\end{array}\right] \\
& =\left[\begin{array}{c}
\boldsymbol{O}_{3} \\
\boldsymbol{H}_{\mathrm{B} 321}\left(\boldsymbol{\theta}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}}\right)

## 体元件速度关于系统广义速度的线性表达式
 ![[体元件前序元件牵连速度系数矩阵.png]]
 对于任意体元件 $i$ ，其绝对速度列阵 $\boldsymbol{v}_{0, i \mid i}$  可记为如下形式 
$$
\boldsymbol{v}_{0, i \mid i}=\sum_{k \in \overrightarrow{\mathrm{p}}[i]} \boldsymbol{C}_{i, k} \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}} \dot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}},
$$
式中牵连速度矩阵：
$$\boldsymbol{C}_{i, k}=\left[\begin{array}{cc}
\boldsymbol{A}_{k, i}^{\mathrm{T}} & \boldsymbol{A}_{k, i}^{\mathrm{T}} \tilde{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_{k} \mathrm{O}_{i} \mid k}^{\mathrm{T}} \\
\boldsymbol{O}_{3} & \boldsymbol{A}_{k, i}^{\mathrm{T}}
\end{array}\right], \quad k \in \overrightarrow{\mathrm{p}}[i],$$
对于任意元件$i$,其牵连速度系数矩阵满足如下递推关系：
$$\boldsymbol{C}_{i, k}=\left\{\begin{array}{ll}
\boldsymbol{I}_{6} & k=i, \\
\boldsymbol{C}_{i, \mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{C}_{\mathrm{p}(i), k} & k \in \overrightarrow{\mathrm{p}}[\overrightarrow{\mathrm{p}}[i]] .
\end{array}\right.$$
下面给出证明：
绝对速度列阵表达式$\boldsymbol{v}_{0, i \mid i}=\sum_{k \in \overrightarrow{\mathrm{p}}[i]} \boldsymbol{C}_{i, k} \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}} \dot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}}$对单一体元件是恒成立的。对于单一体元件，有$\{j\mid \mathrm{p}(j)=0\}$，于是有：
$$\begin{aligned}
\boldsymbol{v}_{0, j \mid j} & =\sum_{k \in \overrightarrow{\mathrm{p}}[j]} \boldsymbol{C}_{j, k} \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}} \dot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}} \\
& =\sum_{k \in\{j\}} \boldsymbol{C}_{j, k} \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}} \dot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}} \\
& =\boldsymbol{C}_{j, j} \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_{j}} \dot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_{j}} \\
& =\boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_{j}} \dot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_{j}},
\end{aligned}$$
假设体元件$\mathrm{p}(i)$绝对速度列阵$\boldsymbol{v}_{0,\mathrm{p}(i)\mid \mathrm{p}(i)}$满足表达式$$\boldsymbol{C}_{i, k}=\left[\begin{array}{cc}
\boldsymbol{A}_{k, i}^{\mathrm{T}} & \boldsymbol{A}_{k, i}^{\mathrm{T}} \tilde{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_{k} \mathrm{O}_{i} \mid k}^{\mathrm{T}} \\
\boldsymbol{O}_{3} & \boldsymbol{A}_{k, i}^{\mathrm{T}}
\end{array}\right], \quad k \in \overrightarrow{\mathrm{p}}[i]$$
即有:$\boldsymbol{v}_{0, \mathrm{p}(i) \mid \mathrm{p}(i)}=\sum_{k \in \overrightarrow{\mathrm{p}}[\mathrm{p}(i)]} \boldsymbol{C}_{\mathrm{p}(i), k} \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}} \dot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}},$
先证明牵连速度矩阵有如下性质：
$$\boldsymbol{C}_{i,\mathrm{p}(i)}\boldsymbol{C}_{\mathrm{p}(i),k}=\boldsymbol{C}_{i,k}$$
将其代入体元件运动学方程$\boldsymbol{a}_{0, i \mid i}=\boldsymbol{C}_{i, \mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{a}_{0, \mathrm{p}(i) \mid \mathrm{p}(i)}+\boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} \ddot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}}+\boldsymbol{\eta}_{i}$,整理可得：
$$\begin{aligned}
\boldsymbol{v}_{0, i \mid i} & =\boldsymbol{C}_{i, \mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{v}_{0, \mathrm{p}(i) \mid \mathrm{p}(i)}+\boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} \dot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} \\
& =\sum_{k \in \overrightarrow{\mathrm{p}}[\mathrm{p}(i)]} \boldsymbol{C}_{i, \mathrm{p}(i)} \boldsymbol{C}_{\mathrm{p}(i), k} \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}} \dot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}}+\boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} \dot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} \\
& =\sum_{k \in \overrightarrow{\mathrm{p}}[\mathrm{p}(i)]} \boldsymbol{C}_{i, k} \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}} \dot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}}+\boldsymbol{C}_{i, i} \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} \dot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_{i}} \\
& =\sum_{k \in\{\overrightarrow{\mathrm{p}}[\mathrm{p}(i)], i\}} \boldsymbol{C}_{i, k} \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}} \dot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}} \\
& =\sum_{k \in \overrightarrow{\mathrm{p}}[i]} \boldsymbol{C}_{i, k} \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}} \dot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}} .
\end{aligned}$$

\boldsymbol{O}_{2 \times 3} & \boldsymbol{T}_{\mathrm{L}_{j}}^{\mathrm{T}} \boldsymbol{A}_{\underline{\underline{\mathrm{b}}}(j), \underline{\mathrm{m}}(j)} \tilde{\boldsymbol{n}}_{\mathrm{L}_{j}}^{\mathrm{T}}
\end{array}\right] \boldsymbol{v}_{\underline{\underline{\mathrm{r}}}(j), \underline{\mathrm{m}}(j) \mid \underline{\mathrm{m}}(j)} \\
& -\left[\begin{array}{cc}
\boldsymbol{A}_{\underline{\mathrm{r}}(j), \underline{\mathrm{b}}(j)} & \boldsymbol{A}_{\underline{\mathrm{r}}(j), \underline{\mathrm{b}}(j)} \tilde{\boldsymbol{r}}_{\mathrm{O}_{\mathrm{b}}(j)}^{\mathrm{T}} \underline{\mathrm{~B}}_{j} \mid \underline{\mathrm{b}}(j) \\
\boldsymbol{O}_{2 \times 3} & \boldsymbol{T}_{\mathrm{L}_{j}}^{\mathrm{T}} \boldsymbol{A}_{\underline{\underline{\mathrm{b}}(j), \mathrm{m}}(j)} \tilde{\boldsymbol{n}}_{\mathrm{L}_{j}}^{\mathrm{T}} \boldsymbol{A}_{\underline{\mathrm{b}}(j), \underline{\mathrm{m}}(j)}^{\mathrm{T}}
\end{array}\right] \boldsymbol{v}_{\underline{\underline{\mathrm{r}}}(j), \underline{\mathrm{b}}(j) \mid \underline{\mathrm{b}}(j)} \\
& =: \boldsymbol{\Psi}_{\underline{\mathrm{m}}(j)} \boldsymbol{v}_{\underline{\underline{\mathrm{r}}}(j), \underline{\mathrm{m}}(j) \mid \underline{\mathrm{m}}(j)}-\boldsymbol{\Psi}_{\underline{\mathrm{b}}(j)} \boldsymbol{v}_{\underline{\underline{\mathrm{r}}}(j), \underline{\mathrm{b}}(j) \mid \underline{\mathrm{b}}(j)} .
\end{aligned}$$

## 速度级约束方程关于系统广义速度的线性表达式

$$\begin{aligned}
\dot{\boldsymbol{c}}_{\mathrm{L}_{j}} & =\boldsymbol{\Psi}_{\underline{\mathrm{m}}(j)} \boldsymbol{v}_{\underline{\mathrm{r}}(j), \underline{\mathrm{m}}(j) \mid \underline{\mathrm{m}}(j)}-\boldsymbol{\Psi}_{\underline{\mathrm{b}}(j)} \boldsymbol{v}_{\underline{\mathrm{r}}(j), \underline{\mathrm{b}}(j) \mid \underline{\mathrm{b}}(j)} \boldsymbol{C}_{\underline{k \in \overrightarrow{\mathrm{p}}}[\underline{\mathrm{~m}}(j)], k>\underline{\mathrm{r}}(j)} \boldsymbol{C}_{\underline{\mathrm{m}}(j), k} \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}} \dot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}} \\
& =\boldsymbol{\Psi}_{\underline{\mathrm{b}}(j)} \sum_{k \in \overrightarrow{\mathrm{p}}[\underline{\mathrm{~b}}(j)], k>\underline{\mathrm{r}}(j)} \boldsymbol{C}_{\underline{\mathrm{b}}(j), k} \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}} \dot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}} \\
& =\sum_{k \in \overrightarrow{\mathrm{p}}[\underline{\mathrm{~m}}(j)], k>\underline{\mathrm{r}}(j)} \boldsymbol{\Psi}_{\underline{\mathrm{m}}(j)} \boldsymbol{C}_{\underline{\mathrm{m}}(j), k} \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}} \dot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}} \\
& =: \sum_{k \in \overrightarrow{\mathrm{p}}[\underline{\mathrm{~b}}(j)], k>\underline{\mathrm{r}}(j)], k>\underline{\mathrm{r}}(j)} \boldsymbol{\Psi}_{\mathrm{L}_{j}, \boldsymbol{q}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}}} \boldsymbol{\dot { q }}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}} \boldsymbol{C}_{\underline{\mathrm{b}}(j), k} \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}} \dot{\boldsymbol{q}}_{\mathrm{J}_{k}} \\

\end{aligned}$$

$$\{k \mid k \in \overrightarrow{\mathrm{p}}[\underline{\mathrm{~m}}(j)], k>\underline{\mathrm{r}}(j)\} \cap\{k \mid k \in \overrightarrow{\mathrm{p}}[\underline{\mathrm{~b}}(j)], k>\underline{\mathrm{r}}(j)\}=\varnothing$$

## 闭环违约修正
导致闭环约束方程出现违约的因素包括初始装配偏差、浮点数舍入误差、微分方程数值解法截断误差以及累积误差等.

### 位置级违约修正
在任意给定时刻 $t$, 针对一组给定的系统广义坐标$\boldsymbol{q}$，当位置级约束方程违约量的按模最大值超过给定的阈值时，即当
$$\|\boldsymbol{c}(\boldsymbol{q}, t)\|_{\infty}>\varepsilon$$
时，需修正系统广义坐标$\boldsymbol{q}$，即寻找系统广义坐标的修正量$\Delta \boldsymbol{q}$,使得修正后的系统广义坐标$\boldsymbol{q}+\Delta \boldsymbol{q}$对应的违约量$\boldsymbol{c}(\boldsymbol{q}+\Delta \boldsymbol{q},t)$满足阈值要求，即：
$$\|\boldsymbol{c}(\boldsymbol{q}+\Delta \boldsymbol{q},t)\|_{\infty}\leq\varepsilon$$
上式为关于$\Delta \boldsymbol{q}$ 的非线性方程组，仍不易直接求解，可进一步利用*Taylor*公式作线性化处理，即
$$\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{0} & =\boldsymbol{c}(\boldsymbol{q}+\Delta \boldsymbol{q}, t) \\
& =\boldsymbol{c}(\boldsymbol{q}, t)+\boldsymbol{c}_{\boldsymbol{q}}(\boldsymbol{q}, t) \Delta \boldsymbol{q}+\boldsymbol{o}(\Delta \boldsymbol{q}) .
\end{aligned}$$
一般情况下，上述关于$\Delta \boldsymbol{q}$的线性方程组有无穷多组解，在这些解中寻求二范数最小的一组解，求解过程不妨简记为如下表达式
$$\Delta \boldsymbol{q}=-\boldsymbol{c}_{\boldsymbol{q}}^{+} \boldsymbol{c}$$
根据求得的系统广义坐标修正量$\Delta \boldsymbol{q}$  更新系统广义坐标，即
$$\boldsymbol{q} \leftarrow \boldsymbol{q}+\Delta \boldsymbol{q} $$
针对更新后的系统广义坐标，再次判定位置级约束方程违约量的按模最大值是否超过给定的阈值，若是则重复上述修正过程，若否则位置级约束方程修正完毕.

### 速度级违约修正
广义速度修正量可由下式直接获得，无迭代过程
$$\Delta\dot{\boldsymbol{q}} =-\boldsymbol{c}_{\boldsymbol{q}}^{+} \dot{\boldsymbol{c}}$$
